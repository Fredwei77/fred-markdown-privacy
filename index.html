Files and content

You can choose Markdown files using your device’s file picker, edit text in the app, and choose where to save converted HTML. The app processes this content on your device. It does not require an account or sign-in and does not include advertising, analytics, or a crash-reporting service.

A file provider you select, such as a cloud storage service, may process or transmit files according to its own terms and privacy policy. The app cannot control that provider. Exported HTML can retain image references; external readers may handle those references differently. The app’s preview does not load images.

App settings and retention

The app stores settings such as your selected language, welcome status and an export-in-progress flag in its private app storage. Android may also retain editor text and selected file information to restore the screen. The app may request continued read access to selected files. Selected files remain with their original providers and exported files remain in the locations you select.

Uninstalling the app removes its private app data, subject to your device’s operating system; it does not automatically delete the source files or files you exported to other locations. You can manage those files through the relevant file provider.

Handling and security

The app uses Android’s file picker to access files and destinations you choose. Its preview disables JavaScript and blocks network, file and content access. The security and retention of files stored with an external provider depend on that provider and your device settings.
